## Types of fits
### Clearance fit
Tolerance allows movements
- Tolerance zones don't intersect

### Transition fit
Easy assembly and dissasembly
- Tolerance zone partially intersect

### Interference fit
Rigid, strong connection. Tolerance zones fully intersect.
- Tolerance zones fully intersect

Note: requires use of materials with greater elongation.
